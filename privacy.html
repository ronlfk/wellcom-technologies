Privacy Policy

This policy explains what our games and this website collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. What we collect

We try to collect as little as the game needs to work. In practice it falls into three groups.

Information you give us

  • An account identifier, if you choose to create or link an account so your progress follows you between devices.
  • Anything you write to us yourself — a support message, a bug report, a survey answer.

Information created by playing

  • Game progress: levels completed, items owned, settings, and where you are in a session.
  • Gameplay events: a level started, failed, retried or finished, and how long it took. We use these to tune difficulty.
  • Purchase records, where you have made a purchase. Payment itself is handled by the app store — we never receive your card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app data: device model, operating system version, language, region, app version.
  • A device or advertising identifier provided by your operating system.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics.
  • On this website: standard server logs, and only the cookies needed for the site to function. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

We do not ask for your real name, your postal address, your contacts, your photos, your microphone or your precise location, and our games do not request those permissions.

2. Why we collect it

  • To run the game — saving progress, restoring it on a new device, and delivering anything you bought.
  • To keep it working — finding crashes, fixing bugs, and spotting cheating or abuse.
  • To make it better — understanding which levels frustrate people, and testing changes behind flags before they ship widely.
  • To answer you — handling support requests and the rights requests described below.
  • To meet legal obligations — tax and accounting records for purchases, and responses to lawful requests.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example the GDPR), we rely on performance of a contract for running the game, our legitimate interest in keeping it stable and improving it, your consent where consent is asked for, and legal obligation for record keeping.

3. Advertising and analytics

Where a game shows advertising, ads appear between sessions and never over a move you are about to make. Advertising and analytics partners may receive your device or advertising identifier and basic device data in order to deliver and measure those ads.

You can limit this at any time. Both iOS and Android let you reset your advertising identifier or turn off personalised advertising in system settings, and our games respect that choice. Where consent is required for personalised advertising, we ask for it in the game and you can change your answer later in the game settings.

We do not sell player data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contract, and only for the purposes above. Those categories are:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers.
  • Crash reporting and analytics providers.
  • Advertising networks, where a game shows ads.
  • App stores, which handle purchases and subscriptions.
  • Customer support tooling.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or to protect the rights and safety of players and our staff. If the company or a game is sold or transferred, player data may move with it, and this policy continues to apply until you are told otherwise.

5. How long we keep it

  • Game progress — for as long as the account is active. If an account has been inactive for a long period, we may delete it along with its progress.
  • Gameplay and diagnostic events — kept in identifiable form for a limited period, then aggregated so individual players can no longer be identified.
  • Support messages — kept for as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up.
  • Purchase records — kept for the period required by tax and accounting law.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what data we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Have your data deleted, including deleting your account and its progress.
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing.
  • Withdraw a consent you previously gave, without affecting what was done before you withdrew it.
  • Ask for your data in a portable format.

We do not charge for these requests, and we do not make the game worse for you because you made one. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Deleting your account deletes your progress with it. That cannot be undone, and progress cannot be restored afterwards from a backup.

7. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Where we know, or are told, that an account belongs to a minor, we turn off personalised advertising and apply spending caps by default. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to production systems is limited to the people who need it for their work, and that access is logged. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data and the law requires us to tell you, we will.

9. International transfers

Our service providers operate in several countries, so your data may be processed outside the country you live in. Where data leaves the EEA or the UK, we rely on the transfer mechanisms permitted by that law, such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the game before it takes effect rather than relying on you to re-read this page.

11. Contacting us

Privacy questions, and all of the requests in section 6, can be sent to us by email, or from the support screen inside any of our games — the in-game route is usually faster, because it identifies your account automatically.
